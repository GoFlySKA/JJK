GOJO VS SUKUNA

Desktop: WASD move · Mouse aim/look · LMB basic attack · Space dodge · Shift sprint · Q Blue · E Red · R Hollow Purple · F Teleport · X Six Eyes · C Infinity · V Unlimited Void · Esc pause.
Mobile: left joystick move · drag right side to aim · buttons on right.

No external game/anime assets are used. All characters and effects are generated with Three.js geometry.
